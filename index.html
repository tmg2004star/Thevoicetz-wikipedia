Identity & Style

Their art is characterized by pop-style arrangements with intricate vocal harmonies, scat singing, riffing, vocal percussion, and beatboxing. They produce cover versions of modern pop works and Christmas songs, along with original material. They exclusively minister as a Gospel Music Ensemble.

The Complete Story

Archived historical timeline (1994 - Present)

CHAPTER 01

Foundation (1994)

Founded by Obeid John Mark alongside his brothers Daniel, Baraka, and Mark from Magomeni Kagera. Originally named DAMOBA Christian Choir by their father, they transitioned to contemporary a cappella and formed The Voice Tanzania in 1994.

CHAPTER 02

The 7-Year Ban (2003)

Approached by the Kilimanjaro Music Awards, their participation created controversy at their home church (Magomeni SDA). This led to a seven-year ban from performing. Obedi cited 1 Corinthians 9:22 to defend reaching audiences outside the church walls.

CHAPTER 03

Generational Shifts (2011 - 2021)

After a highly successful Tigo advertisement that funded their university education, the group evolved. Melisa John joined in 2011. The 2nd and 3rd generations saw members from Vocapella and Heavenly Singers join, establishing a powerful new vocal dynamic.
